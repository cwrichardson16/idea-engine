### Patterns

1. Currencies are plain concrete nouns: Gold, Wood, Stone, Iron, Food, Coins. Invented words are rare; Supercell's Elixir is the main exception and works because it is one short word with a clear color.
2. Two main resources is the genre norm in light base-builders (CoC, Hay Day); the heavier 4X games use four or five. Our game wants two main plus premium plus Knowledge (speed-up), which matches CoC.
3. Premium is always a gem-like word (Gems, Diamonds) or a vanity-coin (Crowns, Magicoin). It is the only slot where a "shiny stone" word is expected.
4. Makers are named after the job plus the material: Gold Mine, Lumber Mill, Sawmill, Quarry, Farm. Storage is Vault, Storage, Granary, Treasury.
5. Core building is a seat of power: Town Hall, Headquarters, City Hall, Furnace. The builder is a small lodge or hut.
6. Defenses are physical siege words: Cannon, Archer Tower, Mortar, Ballista. Keep these literal; players decode them instantly.
7. Troops are job titles or fantasy archetypes, one word each (Archer, Wizard, Giant). Ranks reuse the troop names (CoC) or gem/metal tiers (Duolingo).
8. Game titles: Clash of Clans, Boom Beach, Hay Day, Rise of Kingdoms, Lords Mobile, Whiteout Survival, Kingdom Rush, Castle Clash. Most are either "Verb/Adjective + Noun" (Clash Royale, Boom Beach, Hay Day) or "Noun + of + Noun" or "Noun + Noun" (Kingdom Rush, Castle Clash). Almost all use real English words; no heavy invented words. Two short real words is the sweet spot.
9. No study game themes its currency to the subject (Duolingo, Prodigy, Habitica), so a generic skin fits the prior art.

Open gap: no school/library PvP base-builder turned up in search; the sky-island niche has small indie entries (below).

## 5. Recommendation: Direction A (classic kingdom)

Why: (1) every top game in the scan uses plain medieval or material nouns, and the owner rejected stylized words, so this set has the lowest rejection risk; (2) it is subject-neutral, so a language learner and a law student feel equally at home, and the Knowledge currency carries the study tie by itself, as Duolingo gems and Habitica gold do; (3) PvP raiding castles is the most natural fiction, while a library or an island needs explaining; (4) all names are literal and instantly iconable. Cost: least distinctive. If distinctiveness matters most, Direction B has the strongest study identity and an open niche, but weaker loot icons.

Recommended title: Stonehaven (backup Kingsreach). Search found no conflicting product but this is not a trademark check.
