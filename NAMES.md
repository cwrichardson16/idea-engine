# Naming options (2026-11-28)

Goal: names that read like a polished mobile game (short, concrete nouns, a little stylized), not jokes and not a corporate org chart. Never "Anki"; nothing that echoes Supercell names. Knowledge stays as is. Trademark and store-name conflicts below are from memory and all UNVERIFIED; run a real search before anything public.

Three directions used throughout:
- Clinical-cool: sharp, modern, a bit sci-fi (lab, ward, pulse).
- Heroic medicine: healers as a besieged order (guardians, vanguard, vitality).
- Academic prestige: old-medical-school gravitas (amphitheater, rounds, boards).

## 1. Game title

| Direction | Option | Why | Conflict risk (unverified) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clinical-cool | Scrub In | Keeps the "Scrubs" sound he liked, but it is the real surgical phrase for starting work. Two short words, easy icon. | Close to the TV show "Scrubs" in sound only; likely some small apps use "Scrub In". Medium. |
| Clinical-cool | Pulsehold | "Hold the pulse" plus "stronghold". One word, brandable, game-like. | Probably free as a game; check. Low. |
| Clinical-cool | Ward Zero | Ward = hospital unit and a fortress word; "Zero" is stylish. | Several indie titles use "Zero"; low-medium. |
| Heroic | Aegis Ward | Shield plus hospital ward. Sounds like a real mobile game. | "Aegis" is very common in games/software; medium. |
| Heroic | Ironward | One chunky word; ward as in guarded place. | Low-medium; generic compound. |
| Heroic | Vanguard Medica | Order-of-healers feel. | "Vanguard" is heavily used (Call of Duty Vanguard, the investment firm); high. Skip. |
| Academic | Residency | The real stage of a doctor's life, and "a place you live and defend". Calm, confident. | Common word; some games/apps carry it; medium. |
| Academic | Rounds | Hospital "rounds" and "rounds of battle". Very short. | Very generic, hard to trademark; medium. |
| Academic | Magna Cura | Latin "great healing"; prestige tone. | Probably low; sounds less mobile-game. |

## 2. Currencies

Rule of thumb: Resource 1 should be a hard, shiny, golden-ish thing; Resource 2 a liquid or energy; premium a gem-like crystal. Avoid pink liquid (Elixir) and yellow coin (Gold).

| Slot | Clinical-cool | Heroic medicine | Academic prestige |
|---|---|---|---|
| Resource 1 (builds/upgrades) | Amber (IV-bag amber glass, warm orange chunk) | Alloy (surgical steel, bars) | Endowment (a bit corporate; fallback only) |
| Resource 1 alt | Chrome | Bronze | Ledger (weak) |
| Resource 2 (trains troops) | Serum (teal-green vial glow, not pink) | Vitae (life essence, glowing) | Plasma (straw-gold liquid) |
| Resource 2 alt | Pulse | Adrenaline | Ichor |
| Premium rare | Lumens (glowing crystals) | Heartstones | Opals (or "Sapphires") |
| Premium alt | Isotopes | Panacea | Pearls |

Best sound together: Amber / Serum / Lumens. Short, concrete, nothing joke-like, and each has an obvious icon.

## 4. Troops (cheap melee, ranged, big tank)

| Direction | Melee | Ranged | Tank |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clinical-cool | Orderly | Titrator (throws measured doses) | Attending |
| Heroic | Medic | Lancer (lancets) | Chief |
| Academic | Intern | Apothecary | Professor (or Dean) |

Best trio: Orderly / Apothecary / Attending. Three real hospital roles, all short, none a joke. "Intern" is also fine for the cheap unit if he wants the med-school feel.

## 5. Single-player raid targets (the goblin-village slot)
