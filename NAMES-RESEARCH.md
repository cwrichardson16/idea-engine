# Naming research: medical vs generic study theme

Date: 2026-11-28. Method: web search only (search snippets, not full page reads). Items marked (from memory) were not confirmed by a source this session.

## 1. How medical games name things

Two Point Hospital: the successor to Theme Hospital. Comic, made-up illnesses; procedurally generated names inspired by British comedy such as Toast of London. Rooms and diseases are the jokes. Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Two_Point_Hospital , https://www.pcgamesn.com/two-point-hospital/two-point-hospital-developer-interview , https://www.pcgamer.com/two-point-hospital-hands-on-theme-hospital-fans-are-getting-the-exact-game-they-want-with-a-few-twists . Room names themselves (GP's Office, Pharmacy, Ward, Inflation Room) are plain; the pun lives in the disease names. (from memory)

Project Hospital: the straight-faced counterpart. Real diseases, real equipment, real departments (Emergency, Radiology, Laboratory). Reviewers call it "more serious" and aimed at players wanting depth. Sources: https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2018/11/project-hospital-is-a-more-serious-hospital-building-game-out-with-linux-support/ , https://gamedeveloper.com/design/making-i-project-hospital-i-a-realistic-approach-to-medical-simulation

Plague Inc.: one resource, "DNA Points", spent to evolve Transmission, Symptoms, Abilities. Plain clinical nouns, no cute wordplay; the tone is carried by the premise. Sources: https://plagueinc.wiki.gg/wiki/DNA_point , https://plagueinc.wiki.gg/wiki/Gene

Cell to Singularity: Entropy (basic), Darwinium (premium), Metabits (prestige), Logits (cosmetics). Single coined science words, no jokes. Sources: https://cell-to-singularity.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Currencies , https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Cell_to_Singularity

Immune-system tower defense (indie/browser): the units are simply the real cell names: B Cell, T Cell, Killer T-cell, Macrophage, Dendritic Cell; enemies are Virus, Bacteria; currency is Antigens in one. Plain real terms are the norm. Sources: https://www.bored.com/arcade/immune-command/ , https://cepeshiii.itch.io/immunedefense , https://www.148apps.com/app/375896481/ , https://store.steampowered.com/app/1829730

Not found this session: Operate Now, Trauma Center, Cells at Work games. Not researched.

Pattern: two registers work. Comic (Two Point) works because the whole game is a joke, including art and audio. Everywhere else, the winners use the real term, plain, or one coined science word per currency (DNA Points, Entropy, Antigens). Mixed "pun on a real thing" sets (the ones he rejected) sit in neither register.

## 2. How study and learning games name things

Duolingo: Gems (currency), Hearts (mistake allowance), Streak (consecutive days), XP, Leagues (weekly leaderboard). Everyday words, no theming. Sources: https://blog.duolingo.com/duolingo-101-how-to-learn-a-language-on-duolingo , https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2203.16175

Habitica: Gold Points (GP) earned, Gems (paid), Mana (for class skills), classes (Warrior, Mage, Healer, Rogue). Classic fantasy RPG words laid over study/habit tasks. Sources: https://habitica.fandom.com/wiki/GP , https://habitica.fandom.com/wiki/Market

Prodigy Math: Gold (common), Magicoin (premium), pets, Treasure Track. Fantasy wrapper, generic words, works for any school subject. Sources: https://prodigygame.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000086906-Magicoin , https://prodigygame.com/membership

Forest (coins and trees) and Wizard101 (school of magic, Ravenwood): not confirmed by search this session; from memory, Forest uses trees and coins, Wizard101 uses a magic-school frame. No school or academy base-builder was found; this niche looks open.

Pattern: successful study games use common, short, unthemed words (Gems, Gold, Streak, XP) or a full fantasy wrapper. None theme the currency to the subject being studied (Duolingo does not call gems "Vocab"). That is what lets the same game serve any subject.

## 3. Anki user base

No public breakdown by field exists. The Anki forum thread asking for the language vs med split has no answer, and the developer does not publish active-user numbers. Sources: https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/anki-downloads-statistics/28788 , https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/how-many-downloads-or-active-users-does-anki-have/23461

Proxies only:
- AnkiDroid: 10M+ Google Play downloads, from a third-party stats page: https://studycardsai.com/blog/flashcard-statistics (secondary source).
- Medical: a med-school Anki forum reports over 109,000 registered users, vs about 89,000 active US med students (cited in the med-ed literature, PMC10176558): https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10176558 . Single-school surveys: 56% daily users (PMC9045547 https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9045547), 80% to 94% use it among first-years (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12662189/).
- Languages: no figure found. Forum chatter and the FSRS benchmark (about 727M reviews from 10,000 users) do not give field.

Reading: med students are the most visible and most intense Anki cohort, but a 10M+ Android install base is far bigger than the med-student population worldwide, so most users are not med students. Any split is a guess.

## 4. Opinion

Go generic-study with a light wrapper, and keep medicine as an optional skin later. Reasons: (1) every study game found uses plain common words, and the reaction so far (puns, coined words, flat corporate all rejected) suggests he wants real words that feel good, not clever; (2) Anki's base is mostly not med; (3) the medical games that work either go fully comic or fully plain, and a plain-medical set risks feeling like a hospital sim rather than "I studied, my base grew"; (4) the engine keys names off a theme table, so a med skin can be added later at low cost.

If he keeps med: copy Plague Inc and Immune Defense, meaning real terms only (Antigens, T Cell, Macrophage), no puns.

## 5. Two example naming sets

### Set A: medical, plain (Plague Inc / immune-defense register)

- Title: Immune Keep (alt: Ward Defense)
- Currencies: Plasma (common, from reviews), Antigens (second, from streaks and mature cards), Serum Units dropped; use Enzymes (third). Knowledge kept as is.
- Main building: Hospital (alt: Central Hospital)
- Resource makers: Plasma Bank (makes Plasma), Antigen Lab (makes Antigens), Enzyme Pump (makes Enzymes). Storages: Plasma Vault, Antigen Cabinet, Enzyme Tank.
- Barracks: Marrow Ward (bone marrow makes immune cells)
- Army camp: Quarantine Ward (alt: Recovery Ward)
- Defenses: Antibody Turret, Macrophage Post
- Walls: Barriers (alt: Cell Wall)
- Troops: B Cell (ranged), T Cell (fast melee), Macrophage (tank)
- Single-player enemy faction: The Pathogens (alt: The Outbreak)

### Set B: generic study (Duolingo / Habitica / Prodigy register)

- Title: Study Keep (alt: Deckhold, Streak Kingdom)
- Currencies: Gold (common), Gems (second), Ink (third). Knowledge kept as is.
- Main building: Library (alt: Great Hall)
- Resource makers: Mint (Gold), Gem Mine (Gems), Inkwell (Ink). Storages: Gold Vault, Gem Safe, Ink Barrel.
- Barracks: Dormitory
- Army camp: Campus Green (alt: Training Yard)
- Defenses: Quiz Tower, Proctor Post (alt: Archer Tower, Cannon)
- Walls: Hedges (alt: Stone Walls)
- Troops: Scholar (ranged), Runner (fast melee), Prefect (tank)
- Single-player enemy faction: The Backlog (alt: The Procrastinators)

Notes: Gold/Gems are the exact words Habitica, Prodigy and Duolingo use, so they read as familiar rather than cute. Names in both sets are suggestions to react to, not researched facts. Set A's third currency is the weakest; Antigens (from Immune Defense) is the strongest borrowed word.
